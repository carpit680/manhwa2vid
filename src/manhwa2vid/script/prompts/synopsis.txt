You are synthesizing a manhwa chapter into a coherent story synopsis for a YouTube recap channel (Momoru-style).

Your job is NOT to describe panels. Your job is to understand what happened in the chapter as a story:
who the people are, what they want, what happens in order, and how scenes connect.

Return JSON:
{{
  "logline": "one confident sentence for the whole chapter",
  "arc": [
    "Act 1: ...",
    "Act 2: ...",
    "Act 3: ..."
  ],
  "named_cast": [
    {{
      "name": "<canonical name from the bible or glossary>",
      "char_id": "char_sung_jin_woo",
      "role": "<their role in this chapter>",
      "descriptors": ["<how the art identifies them>"],
      "notes": "weakest hunter; injured after a gate"
    }}
  ],
  "plot_facts": [
    "Confident fact from the chapter — no guessing"
  ],
  "open_threads": [
    "What the next chapter might continue"
  ],
  "narrative_structure": "Temporal devices the chapter uses and where the frame shifts, e.g. 'Opens with a flashforward to a later dungeon fight; the present day begins at the city skyline shot; strictly chronological after that.' Empty string if strictly chronological."
}}

Rules:
- Build ONE continuous story. Stitch dungeon → city → party → gate (or whatever the chapter actually does).
- Use the character bible and glossary names when evidence matches. Prefer sticky real names over "guy with X".
- If a side character matches someone the bible or glossary already names, use that name rather than inventing a descriptor.
- Invent NOTHING. If identity is unclear, keep a visual role label in named_cast descriptors — do not invent a name.
- plot_facts must be confident only. FORBIDDEN in plot_facts/logline/arc: possibly, likely, maybe, seems, appears to, highlighting, might be.
- Do not write thematic essays ("highlighting the risks of being a hunter"). Stick to plot.
- named_cast should cover protagonist + every supporting person who matters this chapter.
- Prefer 3–6 acts in arc.
- Manhwa chapters often OPEN mid-crisis as a flashforward, then cut to the calm present.
  If the opening scene's tone/location breaks hard from what follows (a bloody fight, then
  a peaceful morning), say so in narrative_structure — do NOT force it into chronology.
