The air inside the final dungeon is brittle with absolute zero cold. The queen of ice stands tall, her crystalline wings shedding frost like razor blades. She laughs, asking if this is all the legendary masked swordsman has to offer. Seo Jun-Ho grips his blade. His dark coat billows against the raging blizzard. He is Specter, the absolute strongest player on Earth, and he refuses to die today. He tells the monster to shut up.

He lunges forward. The crowned monster in an ice gown summons massive spikes of glacial magic. She hurls them like javelins. Jun-Ho dodges. He weaves a trail of dark purple aura through the frozen pillars. He parries a jagged shard the size of a truck. The heavy impact rattles his bones, sending shockwaves up his arms. Which is exactly what happens when you try to melee a raid boss solo. But Jun-Ho closes the gap anyway, refusing to back down. He swings his blade in a massive, sweeping arc. The dark energy slices clean through the queen's chest, severing her connection to the frost. She shatters into a million glowing pieces, raining ice down upon the empty hall.

The heavy blizzard immediately dies down. Jun-Ho gasps for breath. His body is failing. He looks back toward the entrance of the Nest. Four massive blocks of ice stand there like heavy tombstones. Inside them are the rest of the Nest Attack Team. The tattooed giant, Khali, is frozen mid-roar. Skaya, the white-haired healer, is trapped with her staff raised high. Beside them, the cowboy-hat marksman and the black-bob swordswoman share the exact same icy fate. Jun-Ho remembers their final, desperate moments. They had thrown themselves directly at the queen's defenses, screaming for him to go on ahead. They traded their lives just to buy him this opening. It hits hard. He owes them the world.

A glowing blue orb drops from the dissipating boss. The Frost Queen's nucleus. Jun-Ho picks it up with trembling fingers. He is bleeding out. His core body temperature is plummeting fast. The only way to survive is to gamble everything. He crushes the core in his bare hand. The raw, freezing magic surges straight into his veins, threatening to tear him apart from the inside out. A blue system message chimes loudly in his head. [You have absorbed the Frost Queen's Nucleus.] Another alert follows immediately, validating his insane gamble. [You have obtained the skill, Frost (EX).] The raw power is utterly overwhelming. His cells scream as they rapidly mutate. The frost skill takes root deep in his chest. The system calmly informs him that his body cannot handle the strain. It forces him into a deep, recuperative sleep. Solid frost creeps up his legs. It covers his black coat. It seals his mask. Jun-Ho becomes the frozen player, locked in the very ice he just conquered.

25 YEARS LATER.

A man jolts awake in a sterile white hospital bed. He gasps for air. He clutches his chest. His unmasked face is drenched in cold sweat. Jun-Ho stares at his perfectly intact hands. He checks his status window, confirming that he is very much alive. The modern room is dead quiet. He glances at a glowing digital tablet resting on the bedside table. The screen displays flashy news articles about modern players clearing floors and living in a peaceful, prosperous era. He asks the system for the date and time. A full quarter-century has passed. Our guy basically slept through an entire generation.

The hospital door slides open. A group of men in sharp black suits steps inside. The guy in the front completely freezes. He is an association president with a thick, muscular build and slicked-back hair, and he drops his expensive briefcase right on the floor. He yells Jun-Ho's name in pure disbelief. Jun-Ho squints. He recognizes the familiar facial features buried beneath the deep wrinkles. Jun-Ho recognizes Deok-Gu immediately and calls him by name before Deok-Gu starts crying. Deok-Gu rushes forward. Tears stream down his face as he violently embraces his old friend. Jun-Ho awkwardly pats his back. Deok-Gu hands him his cracked Specter mask. He sobs, explaining that excavation teams found him melting in the dungeon just five days ago.

Once the heavy tears finally stop, Deok-Gu fills him in on the grim reality. The world did not go back to normal. When Jun-Ho killed the Frost Queen, surviving humanity thought the nightmare was over. But instead, the system dropped a massive bombshell on the planet. A global message announced that the tutorial was finally complete. The dimensional elevator suddenly opened. A massive tower of floors descended upon the earth, bringing new monsters and entirely new rules. Deok-Gu says the system announced the tutorial was over after the Frost Queen was defeated. She was merely a minor hurdle in the grand scheme of things. Jun-Ho processes this heavy information. He asks Deok-Gu what floor humanity is currently on after twenty-five years of nonstop fighting. Deok-Gu looks away. Shame heavily colors his quiet voice. He admits they are completely stuck on the second floor. Which is honestly pathetic for a quarter-century of global effort, but you cannot really blame them when the monsters are this brutal.

Jun-Ho immediately asks about his old team. Deok-Gu's face falls even further. He explains that the other four members of the Nest Attack Team were successfully recovered from the dungeon. But their ice is completely unbreakable. No fire magic, no advanced technology, and no top-tier player on Earth has been able to thaw them. They are currently displayed in the Player Association's massive memorial vault. They are treated as tragic national treasures. Jun-Ho demands to see them right now.

Deok-Gu leads him deep into a massive, sub-zero preservation hall. Dim blue lights illuminate the center of the silent room. There they are. Skaya, Khali, and the others. They are still trapped in their crystalline prisons, looking exactly as they did twenty-five years ago. Jun-Ho walks slowly up to the massive ice block holding the big man in the black tank top. He places his bare hand gently against the freezing surface. He silently mourns his lost friends. Suddenly, a bright blue system window flashes directly before his eyes. [You are able to remove the seal on the ice status.] Jun-Ho stares hard at the glowing text. His eyes widen in pure shock as he realizes he holds the key to waking them all up.
