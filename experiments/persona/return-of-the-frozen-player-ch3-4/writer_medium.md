If you are new to this kind of world, the elevator is essentially a massive tower of floors, each containing its own environments and dungeons, and climbing it is the only way humanity reclaims its territory. With this scaling effect, Jun-Ho knows he can surpass his old limits. He looks at his frozen comrades, locked in their icy sleep. He vows to get them out.

Deok-gu tells him to throw the Specter name away and live simply as Seo Jun-Ho.
