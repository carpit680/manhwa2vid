He is an E-Rank hunter, a title that barely means anything in this brutal world. In fact, he is widely known as the World's Weakest. He never expected his life to end like this, crushed beneath the boots of stone giants. 

Before the statues, he is just a guy trying to survive. At a dusty construction site in Seoul, a glowing blue gate rips through the air. Hunters gather outside in the morning chill. This is their job. They risk their lives for a paycheck, stepping into alternate dimensions to kill monsters. Jin-Woo needs that paycheck to cover his sick mother's medical bills. He has no special talent, no immense strength, and no rich family to lean on.

Mr. Song steps up to the crowd, asking to lead the party for this raid. The others quickly agree. He is the highest-ranked hunter present, and they trust his experience. They step through the swirling blue gate, leaving the construction site behind.

Then, a hunter shouts from the back of the cavern. He found another entrance hidden deep in the shadows. It is a Double Lair. The hunters stare down a dark, ominous tunnel. Every eye turns to Jin-Woo. The World's Weakest holds the deciding vote for the entire raid party. He grips his tiny, cheap magic core tight in his fist. He thinks about his empty wallet. I have to admit, the writing stumbles a bit here by having seasoned hunters completely ignore the blaring warning signs of a glowing ritual room filled with armed giants. Jin-Woo stares past the crowd, his eyes locking onto the colossal statue seated on a throne at the far end of the chamber. It is easily ten times larger than the rest.
